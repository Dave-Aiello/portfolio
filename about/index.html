About

I design web and mobile products. I like to start by talking with the people who'll use them, and I stay involved through launch and after.

I came to design as a founder. In 2014 I started Token Labs, which built crypto payment tools for businesses and a wallet for consumers. Since 2020 I've run Magic Dragon, delivery software for licensed cannabis stores, and I've designed all of it: the customer website, the dispatch board, the back office and the driver app.

Running a company taught me to think past the screen in front of me: the rules we have to follow, the support calls a confusing screen causes, and what happens when a payment fails in the middle of a busy shift. I work closely with engineering, and I use AI tools every day to turn an idea into something people can click through, often the same day.
