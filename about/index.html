About

I design products for places where mistakes are expensive: moving money, buying crypto, checking out an order that needs an ID at the door.

I came to design as a founder. In 2015 I started Token Labs, which built crypto payment tools for businesses and a consumer wallet. Today I run a delivery software platform and make its design decisions: the customer storefront and checkout, a driver app for collecting payment and signatures at the door, and the dispatch board.

Founding taught me to design with the whole business in view: compliance rules, support load, what happens when a payment fails at 9pm. I work closely with engineering, and I use AI tools every day to go from an idea to something people can click on the same afternoon.
