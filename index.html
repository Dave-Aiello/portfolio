Magic Dragon for iPhone

The app customers use to order from Magic Dragon stores. Every order needs proof of age and an ID check at the door, and the app can't advertise discounts. It asks for age and location in one step, and order tracking never shows another customer's stop.

The Main Vine: family trees for giant pumpkins

Giant pumpkin growers choose seeds based on a plant's family history, but the official results only list the parents as typed-in names. I built a site that links every result since 2005 into family trees you can search and click through.

Steady: buying crypto for the first time

A concept for adding crypto to an everyday money app without scaring off people who've never bought it. The price is held while you review, the fee shows before you confirm with Face ID, and the app checks the address before any coins are sent.
