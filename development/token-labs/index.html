Token Labs wallet

Token Labs made crypto payments workable for businesses. Its main product was a full-featured web wallet: several cryptocurrencies in one account, in the browser, with the customer holding their own keys.

Type
Real product, 2014–2020
My role
Founder and Managing Director; front end
Stack
jQuery, Bootstrap, JavaScript
Where
Boston, MA

What it did

  • Held multiple cryptocurrencies in one web-based wallet.
  • Non-custodial storage: customers kept control of their keys.
  • Multi-signature security, so moving funds could require more than one approval.

Token Labs was first to market with non-custodial multi-coin storage and multi-sig security, and became a software provider and partner for businesses taking payments in crypto.

My part

I founded the company, led the team and the product, and built the wallet's front end with jQuery, Bootstrap and plain JavaScript. Token Labs also published its JSON-RPC client libraries for talking to coin daemons as open source: coin-rpc-client and json-rpc-http.

Outcome

The company's assets were acquired by a leading crypto company.
